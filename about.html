About Peakora

Your Journey Into the Himalayas Starts Here

Peakora Treks & Expedition is a Nepal-based adventure travel company creating meaningful journeys through the Himalayas. From iconic routes such as Everest Base Camp and Annapurna to peaceful trails away from the crowds, we help travelers experience Nepal through its mountains, culture and people.

Our team combines local knowledge, experienced guides and thoughtful trip planning to make every adventure smooth, comfortable and memorable. Whether this is your first Himalayan trek or another chapter in your Nepal story, we take care of the details so you can focus on the journey.

Local knowledge. Personal journeys. Discover Nepal with people who know its trails, communities and seasons.
10+ Years of creating memorable Himalayan journeys
What Drives Us

Our Mission & Vision

We believe the best journeys connect you with more than a destination. They introduce you to new landscapes, cultures, people and experiences that stay with you.

Our Mission

To create safe, authentic and thoughtfully planned adventures that allow travelers to discover Nepal with confidence. Through personal service, reliable guidance and careful preparation, we aim to make every journey enjoyable while respecting the people, culture and environments we travel through.

Our Vision

To be a trusted choice for Himalayan adventure travel, connecting travelers with Nepal's extraordinary mountains, communities and culture. We want every guest to return home with unforgettable memories, a deeper appreciation for Nepal and a desire to explore again.
